Privacy Policy

Silverline Games · Applies to Silverline, Silverline 2: The Glass Tide, Silverline 3 and this website · Last updated 28 September 2026

Summary. Our games do not collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.

What our games store on your device

To remember your progress, each game saves a small amount of data on your own device only:

  • your last checkpoint and stage,
  • your coins, suits and techniques from the Workshop,
  • your chosen difficulty and sound setting.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

In-app purchases

If a game offers in-app purchases, the payment is processed entirely by Google Play (or the Apple App Store on iPhone and iPad) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card, bank or UPI details. Google or Apple may share basic order information with us, such as an order ID, the item bought and the date, so that we can deliver the item and answer refund requests. We use it for nothing else.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security, under its own privacy policy.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone.

Children

Our games are suitable for a general audience and collect no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data from the games, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. To delete game progress, clear the app's data or uninstall it. To have an email conversation deleted, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
